A new Docker image for building models with version 2026-10-27 of `Detectron2 <https://github.com/facebookresearch/detectron2>`__ is available.

This image now offers the ability to slice the image internally and combine the annotations again before outputting the predictions.

More information on the Docker image is available from Github:

`github.com/waikato-datamining/pytorch/tree/master/detectron2/2026-10-27 <https://github.com/waikato-datamining/pytorch/tree/master/detectron2/2026-10-27>`__
